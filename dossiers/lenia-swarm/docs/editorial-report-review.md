- Obstacle response: corrected “30% of sham speed” to forward progress. The recorded ratio measures progress, not instantaneous speed.
- Obstacle response: removed the unsupported assertion that a uniformly repelled point mass could not produce the observed directional mixture. The experiment has no such baseline. Shape, internal flow and handedness are hypotheses; mirrored starts test directional reversal but do not by themselves isolate all three.

More substantially, replaced its stale positive account of fish-near transport with the stricter checks: 109/4,802 preliminary groups, one of five broad-corpus candidates surviving, none of five fish-near candidates surviving. ## Imported causal corrections

Exact source/replacement strings are in `editorial-causal-replacements.json`. Apply them through the existing verified editorial projection, not by editing sealed public HTML or changing its receipts manually.

- Spatial-scale report: greater pairwise separation of a fixed panel of outcomes does not count the number of possible futures. Replace that interpretation in the headline, verdict and explanation with measured outcome separation.
- Spatial-scale report: a proposed rescue could support a causal role for spatial relationships; it would not automatically establish an order parameter for developmental commitment.
- Hardening report: the fresh confidence interval includes both zero and the earlier estimate. Its observed width alone does not establish how often a replication would detect a true effect. Remove the unsupported power assertion while retaining the unconfirmed result.
- Recovery report: “current founder fractions passage 0, passage 1, passage 2” confuses founder coordinate indices with time. Use “current fractions of founders 0, 1, and 2.”

## Publication judgment and remaining source limitations

Keep the three principal reports and the seven selected causal accounts. Each has a distinct experimental question; their negative controls and cohort restrictions are part of the account. The revised Fiber Theory page should serve as a companion explanation, not as a second stronger version of the morphology result.

The historical Anatomical Compiler source directory named in its manifest is absent from this checkout. The manifest explicitly leaves the historical producing revision unknown. The report correctly presents its regressions as descriptive: the displayed activity and locomotion R² values must not be promoted elsewhere to held-out or lineage-generalizing predictive performance without checking the original training and evaluation protocol. The selected causal source-result directories are likewise not bundled next to their public pages; receipts establish binding, not this review's independent access to all underlying trajectories. These are limitations of numerical re-verification, not grounds to silently erase recorded findings.

Before claiming an independent scientific reproduction, retrieve the exact hash-bound source results, plans and code. For the present editorial pass, preserve those disclosures and avoid upgrading the status of any result. Desktop/mobile figure inspection is handled separately by the visual reviewer. No claim is made here that every historical report, figure, or source experiment has been reproduced.
