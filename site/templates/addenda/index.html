A-009 / K Semantics Reference

How much search does a policy save?

The start, goal, legal moves, and cost unit stay fixed. A blind walk and a shortest-path policy take different routes through the same problem.

The paths are two seeded replays. K = log₁₀(τ_blind/τ_agent) comes from a separate exact calculation that caps each walk at 500 steps, so its value of 1.22, about 16.6 times less expected cost, is a lower bound for this grid.

A-010 / Equational Theories Distillation

A countermodel you can inspect

We condensed the known implications among 4,694 equational laws into a short sheet a model can use offline. Small operation tables give explicit counterexamples when one law does not imply another, and rules keyed to the starting law prove some of the cases where it does.
