How often does the search still find a proof?

Across 347 completed research runs, we recorded 8,839 tactic-removal reruns. For the survival comparison, 928 had both a successful original search and a successful unchanged control. Of those, 406 still found a proof after the tactic was removed.

131 of the surviving searches produced a different proof hash. The goal stayed fixed while the proof changed—the same pattern illustrated by the alternate route above.

Different proofs can depend on the same tactic

Different-looking proofs can depend on the same indispensable tactic. Removing that tactic tests which alternatives the search can still reach within its budget. A failed rerun does not establish that no other proof exists.
