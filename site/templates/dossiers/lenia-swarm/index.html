Flow Lenia’s local rules move matter across a grid, and some of what they produce holds together as a moving body while the rest breaks apart. We save states and replay them under changed conditions to find out what keeps a body together and which changes send its development somewhere else.

One frame of Orbium was enough to recover its rule

We gave a search one frame of Orbium, the best-known creature of standard Lenia, told it the kind of rule and the starting cells, and hid the three numbers that set the rule’s reach and growth. It came back within six percent of the native value on each. Across 8,155 Flow Lenia creatures, eight shape measurements, mostly size and elongation, explain about half of how far and how fast a body travels, while more complex bodies recover no better from a cut than simple ones.
