What recurs
across different
systems?

We investigate whether different kinds of systems share principles of organization: how a body takes shape, how a collective finds a solution, how a pattern survives disruption. We build experiments in living systems and computational worlds to uncover those principles.

— Manfred Eigen, recalling a 1969 meeting in Versailles

How much does shape explain?

A map of Lenia forms lets us trace gaps and neighborhoods in the collection. To ask whether routes through that map correspond to changes a body can undergo, we also have to follow the dynamics that produce it.

Recovering the rule behind a Lenia body

One observed frame of Orbium was enough for a search to recover its rule close to the native values, and the recovered rule keeps the body coherent well past the frame it was fitted to.

The search scored only the step-600 field, and the recovered rule keeps the body coherent through step 1,200.

Proof search with a missing tactic

We block one tactic from a successful Lean proof search and rerun it under the same budget to see whether the prover can still reach the theorem by another route.
