What recurs
across different
systems?

We investigate whether different kinds of systems share principles of organization: how a body takes shape, how a collective finds a solution, how a pattern survives disruption. We build experiments in artificial life, algorithms, and mathematical search to uncover those principles.

Explore the investigations ↘
“We realised that my equations describing molecular evolution were similar – and some almost identical to those left over on the blackboard by Hermann Haken. Could it be that things as different as laser modes and DNA resemble one another so closely in their dynamic behaviour?”

— Manfred Eigen, recalling a conversation with Hermann Haken

That possibility is part of what draws us to this work. A resemblance can suggest a common mechanism. Following it means finding the right measurements, changing the system, and seeing whether the connection survives.
