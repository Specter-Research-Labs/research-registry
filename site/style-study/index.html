How much does
shape explain?

Comparing Flow Lenia,
simulated tissue, and fish.

We measured the shapes of 25,167 patterns generated by Flow Lenia, then compared them with measurements of real fish. A group chosen before looking at the fish data had unexpectedly similar proportions.

How much can that resemblance tell us about what a body will do?

Interpretation

A match in shape is a beginning.

Similar proportions give us a comparison to investigate. They do not establish a shared mechanism, or tell us how a body will respond to an obstacle.
