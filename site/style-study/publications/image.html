How much does shape explain?

Comparing Flow Lenia, simulated tissue, and fish

We measured the shapes of 25,167 patterns generated by Flow Lenia, then compared them with measurements of real fish. A group chosen before looking at the fish data had unexpectedly similar proportions.

The comparison uses twelve normalized measurements. The selected group contains 256 observations. Its median distance to the nearest fish is 3.30, compared with 5.90 across the full Lenia sample.

These are distances in a space of measurements. They do not mean that the simulated patterns have fish anatomy, or that the same process produced them. The next question is whether similar shapes respond similarly when disturbed.

The report follows that question from the shape map to a separate experiment with moving bodies and obstacles.
