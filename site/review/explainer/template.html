01 / Compare shapes

Where does the resemblance hold?

These measurements compare one Flow Lenia specimen with its nearest fish and simulated-tissue neighbours. A smaller difference means a closer match on that measurement. Sort the rows, then select a landmark to locate the same measured point on four fish.

Similarity gives us a comparison to investigate. To test a response, we need bodies that keep moving long enough to meet an obstacle. The next experiment uses a separate group of five persistent movers.
