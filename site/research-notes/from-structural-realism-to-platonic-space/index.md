# Structural Realism Is The Guardrail

Morphogenesis, proof search, and synthetic worlds keep producing regularities that look less tied to their substrate than we expected. That does not license calling every durable pattern Platonic. First find out what survives when the representation, scale, or intervention changes; a regularity that only exists under one convenient decoding is probably a property of the decoding.

Structural realism ([Ladyman and Ross 2007](https://doi.org/10.1093/acprof:oso/9780199276196.001.0001); [French 2014](https://doi.org/10.1093/acprof:oso/9780199684847.001.0001)) draws a hard line: recurrence is not evidence for a nonphysical realm. The same structure has to show up through more than one measurement channel and survive a perturbation that could have broken it.

“Platonic space” is the much stronger story: patterns live in a structured nonphysical space, and bodies somehow point into it. It differs from ordinary attractor models or multiscale control only where those models fail to predict a result. “Interface” remains a metaphor; [Spivak (2022)](https://topos.institute/people/david-spivak/Levin20220607.pdf) offers polynomial functors for state-dependent interfaces, while Levin's [TAME framing (2022)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.10346) names the kind of persistence under discussion.
