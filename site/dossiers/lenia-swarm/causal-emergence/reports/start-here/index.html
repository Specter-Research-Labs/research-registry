Flow Lenia Causal Emergence: A Guide

We have been using Flow Lenia as a world where a formless seed can become a temporally consistent, coherent individual, then asking a more demanding question than “when does it look alive?”: when does the developing system begin to constrain its own possible futures and the meaning of local interventions?

Evidence snapshot: the synthesis-v4 campaign plus its completed four-run closing programme. The newer impedance, controller-tournament, and causal-passport experiments are deliberately excluded because they were still underway when this page was frozen. No result from those runs appears here.

Development builds causal individuality.

Histories become easier to tell apart and the same push loses leverage, even while sibling futures keep spreading out. Commitment here looks like individuation plus rigidity, not convergence on one shape.

If you read one paragraph

Flow Lenia is a mass-transport variant of Lenia: continuous fields move material around under local rules, and a simple artificial square can reorganize into a persistent body. We watch the state using past-only information measures, freeze a prediction or local action, and only then open the future. A prospective 64-specimen test found that whole-over-parts predictive organization rises before a sustained visible departure from the square. Later causal experiments found that action meanings stabilize and a standard local push moves older organisms less. The surprise is what did not happen: sibling futures widen with age, and a dedicated fine-shape confirmation was unresolved. The system is not collapsing into one canonical adult; it is becoming a more history-specific individual that is harder to redirect.

Four things to know before the numbers.

This is the smallest useful map of the world, the measurement, the interventions, and the claim. The technical labels remain in the report because they identify exact analyses; none are prerequisites for reading it.

01

The world

A Flow Lenia state is a continuous spatial field with several founder channels. The material moves; the developmental pattern reorganizes it. We call a persistent non-square form an organism here, without implying autonomous reproduction.

02

The question

Does a coordinated whole become causally meaningful before the body is obvious? “Meaningful” means measurable in forecasts and in how matched local pokes alter later futures, not merely visually suggestive.

03

The instrument

We compare what the whole past predicts with what the strongest independent split predicts. We also launch a fixed book of local actions from the same state and measure the resulting fan of futures.

04

The answer so far

Organization appears early, then responsiveness falls and identities separate. The evidence supports selective causal commitment. It does not support Φ as a magic steering knob or development as global convergence.

Can the whole forecast itself better than its best split?

The same short past is forecast two ways. One model keeps all cross-part relationships. The other cuts the state at its strongest independent bipartition. Their difference is our whole-over-parts coordinate.

The prospective precursor rose by +0.364 before visible form, but the absolute whole−sum level stayed negative: the whole was catching up with its strongest split, not overtaking it. Adding the score to ordinary visible features barely changed held-out event AUC, from 0.8089 to 0.8093.

Harder to move does not mean closer together.

We expected mature sibling futures to occupy a narrower basin. The frozen test resolved decisively in the other direction. That did not flatten the programme; it told us what kind of commitment we were actually seeing.

The cleaner interpretation Individuality, not one attractor.

Development makes a standard local push less consequential while making sibling trajectories more different from one another. The same visible present also stops admitting as many cross-history look-alikes. A mature organism is therefore more locked into its own history, not more similar to every sibling.

A causal route into organismhood.

The work is interesting because it turns “this looks like an organism” into interventions with competing predictions. We can ask when the whole begins to govern what local actions mean, what becomes harder to change, and which degrees of freedom remain open.

What we would share

A Flow Lenia organism becomes organized first as a constraint on possible futures and action meanings. With age it becomes harder to redirect and more identifiable by its own history, even while siblings diverge.

The GARD connection

GARD supplied the provocation: look for collective predictive organization before familiar organism-level behavior. We use a documented Flow Lenia whole-over-parts analogue; we are not claiming to reproduce an undisclosed estimator from another system.

What remains open

We have not shown autonomous reproduction, a universal scalar of organismhood, or confirmed fine-shape convergence. The action-world and same-state spatial-ablation tests provide real causal handles; none of the evidence summarized here establishes Φ-directed pushes as a general steering policy.

The recurring language, in ordinary words.

Keep these six terms and the rest of the dossier becomes legible.

Flow Lenia
A mass-transport variant of Lenia: continuous spatial fields reorganize under local update rules while material is transported rather than created as ordinary additive growth.
passage 60 / age 60
Developmental time in simulation steps or pulses. Early, turn, and late are organism-relative when the experiment uses each organism's own past-only structural turn.
whole−sum / Φ
The predictive information in the coordinated whole minus the strongest prediction available after splitting it into independent parts. Here it is a partial coordinate, not a consciousness claim.
action-world
The fan of futures produced by applying the same fixed book of local pokes to matched copies of one state.
future-blind / frozen
The selection rule, action, analysis, or pass condition was fixed from past data before the relevant future outcomes were opened.
family
The usual statistical unit: organisms from a shared founder lineage are averaged within family before uncertainty is calculated, so 64 organisms often means 16 independent family units.
