Individual responses before coherent bodies

A developing Lenia body becomes less sensitive to the same disturbance, even as runs with the same rule grow into different shapes. To understand that combination, we follow how its responses change before and after a coherent body appears.

multi-experiment synthesis2026-08-30

Question

How does development change the way a Lenia body responds to its surroundings?

Result

The same disturbance has less influence later in development, while differences among sibling forms grow. Earlier fields already respond in ways that distinguish their source. Together, these experiments make response to intervention part of the description of a body, alongside its visible shape.

Next question

Does a response fingerprint distinguish different life histories under the same rule, and can feedback produce a state that maintains itself after release?
