04 / The fission bridge

Ending at the surface opens a daughter-state window.

For immediate entry into a ≥5%-mass secondary-body state, 0.5→4 minus 4→0.5 is -22.9 percentage points [-40.6, -4.2]. In plain words: the path ending in the low topological dose is more likely to enter the daughter-like state than the path ending in the high expansion dose, after removing both single-dose effects.

The same order that leaves the response near the surface also makes transient macroscopic separation more accessible. That is our first direct bridge from information history → density topology → daughter-state entry.
