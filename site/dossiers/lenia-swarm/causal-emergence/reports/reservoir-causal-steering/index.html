Policy Changes Composition Without Establishing Restoration

The maxPhi worlds produced more restoration onsets than minPhi, and two secondary trajectory measures separated cleanly. But the two predeclared primary links did not: the first post-action Phi shift and the fixed-horizon restoration reward both remained uncertain.

So the honest result is: something in the policy changed the compositional dynamics, but we did not show that integrated predictive information predicts or causes restoration.

Frozen claim decision The causal chain was not earned.

Why later Phi cannot rescue the claim

It averages different post-treatment follow-up sets. Before outcomes were opened, amendment v1a demoted this metric to descriptive and replaced it with the exact first post-action checkpoint.

Why same-cycle ΔH cannot rescue the claim

It only uses cycles observed and valid in both arms. That survivor-conditioned comparison cannot stand in for the unconditional onset reward, where every no-onset terminal remains zero.

The useful interpretation: parcel choice has real leverage over later information structure and local compositional recovery. We still do not know whether Phi is the operative ingredient, or whether these policies are exploiting some broader dynamical feature.

05 / What was measured

The experiment in normal words.

This is about restoration of a four-founder mixture after external passage—not a creature splitting itself.

qP

Parent

The four-founder composition just before passage.

→
qD

Daughter

The composition just after external passage disrupts it.

→
qR

Return

The composition when externally fed growth reaches the next threshold.

ΔH = similarity(qP, qR) − similarity(qP, qD)

What Phi-R asks

How much next-state prediction is lost when the four-founder composition is split into parts? Higher excess Phi-R means more predictive structure belongs to the whole mixture beyond shuffled-future fits.

What onset asks

After not being restored at passage 25, does the lineage later achieve a sustained, predeclared run of composition-restoration episodes before the campaign ends?

08 / Boundary

What this result actually says.

We can say

  • The 1,920-run campaign completed and the receipt chain verifies.
  • Reservoir parcel choice has measurable leverage over these externally maintained compositional lineages.
  • The maxPhi direction produced an interesting onset-count pattern worth a new, separately frozen hypothesis.
  • The strongest resolved effects were later average Phi and survivor-conditioned same-cycle ΔH.

We cannot say

  • That Phi naturally foreshadows restoration in Flow Lenia.
  • That raising Phi caused unconditional restoration onset.
  • That the effect is specific to Phi rather than general predictability or another policy-correlated feature.
  • That this is autonomous self-replication: feeding and passage are supplied from outside.
  • That Phi-R is a universal or primitive cause of causal emergence.
The clean next question: can a fresh cohort, designed around the resolved long-run policy divergence but with a common unconditional observation schedule, distinguish Phi-specific steering from general predictive control? This page does not answer that question retroactively.
