04 / What did not stay universal

The body compass is robust. The wider-futures effect is conditional.

Our previous passage 40 cohort showed forward opening a wider local repertoire around +256. Across this larger four-age map, that average branch-spread effect is small and not tied to one universal age.

What survived

The native temporal direction repeatedly controls later morphology. That result now spans three cohorts and, here, four early ages.

What became more interesting

Future openness is not a generic consequence of moving forward. It may emerge only in particular organisms or near particular spontaneous transitions. That makes it a state-dependent phenomenon to locate, not a scalar to average away.

05 / The next experiment

Find the birth of the arrow.

If the native direction is already causal at passage 24, the next question is not “which later checkpoint is best?” It is: when does this compass first become real, and what visible reorganization follows its appearance?

Next: move earlier—passage 8, passage 12, passage 16, passage 20, passage 24—measure when a prefix-derived direction first beats an equal-energy orthogonal direction, and follow that moment into the first coherent body, first component consolidation, first identity turnover, or first fission-like event.

What a sharp onset would mean

A pre-morphological phase transition: the system acquires a privileged direction of becoming before its body makes that organization obvious.

What a gradual onset would mean

A developmental order parameter: causal directionality accumulates continuously as the initially loose ecology becomes an organism-like whole.
