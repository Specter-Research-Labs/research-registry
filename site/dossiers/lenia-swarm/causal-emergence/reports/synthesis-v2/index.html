Early Organization and the Search for Replication

We followed Levin’s GARD clue into Flow Lenia looking for organization before obvious replication. Two prospective results now converge: the whole narrows its forecasting gap with its best split before committed organism formation, and a separate structural peak precedes reorganization of possible futures.

The newest prospective result A frozen whole-over-parts signal rose by +0.364 before a certified organism-like seed break.

The 95% interval was entirely positive, the direct Φ-R companion rose too, and 15 of 16 fresh family means pointed upward.

What we have now: one fresh prospective precursor to a certified morphogenesis event and one prospective fixed-panel precursor to future-map reorganization. Both were chosen from past-only structure. The decisive next move is intervention: advance or suppress the precursor and watch the biological event move.
01 / idea

The Levin clue was bigger than one number

The GARD paper asks whether a chemical system becomes organized at a higher level before recognizable self-replication arrives. We translated that into Flow Lenia in three increasingly direct views.

01

Scalar integration

Do four founder-composition channels predict their next state better as one coordinated whole than after we split them into parts? Our excess Φ-R score was the first instrument.

02

Decomposition architecture

Which split is weakest, when does that split change, when does the estimator cross a boundary, and how does the covariance geometry reorganize? The parts themselves can move even when the scalar barely does.

03

Future repertoire

From one exact state, launch several matched futures. Their pairwise similarities form a map. If a structural event precedes a change in that map, the system’s organization is beginning to constrain possibility itself.

Φ-R was a lens. The shape of possible futures became the object.

The earlier work becomes legible in that frame. The scalar experiments showed that information organization can be driven; the branching and wound experiments exposed structure hidden from one magnitude; the prospective repertoire experiment joined those lessons into a fresh prediction.

02 / journey

Every attempt changed the question

Each experiment redrew the object: recurrence replaced silhouette fission, intervention replaced correlation, and the geometry of several possible futures replaced one scalar gauge.

I

Find a phenotype

  1. Injury and recovery A founder lineage could be depleted and sometimes return, but the early Φ-R effect changed sign across creature families.
  2. Reservoir recurrence Four-founder compositions really did recur across repeated growth and passage. That gave us a cleaner GARD-like event than silhouette fission.
  3. Temporal exploration Broad recovery did not track scalar integration, yet all three measurable strict onsets showed a pre-onset rise. Timing became the lead.
II

Push the information

  1. Broad steering Policies changed later compositional dynamics, but the intended first-step Φ separation was uncertain.
  2. Orthogonal pulse A cleaner five-arm intervention moved excess Φ-R by +0.188 at passage 41. One passage after release, the contrast was −0.013.
  3. New object We had found a controllable, actively maintained information mode—not a self-sustaining restoration switch.
III

Open the geometry

  1. Branching futures 1,152 trajectories showed that most future fans expanded. Scalar Φ-shape did not predict the rare canalizing cases.
  2. Architecture atlas The only two positive canalization outcomes occurred among 25 estimator-boundary groups; none occurred in 119 ordinary groups.
  3. Connected wounds Equal removed mass produced unequal injury, while nearly equal morphological wounds sometimes required 2.64× different mass. Susceptibility had direction.
IV

Map possibility

  1. Repertoire atlas Eight matched futures became an 8×8 similarity kernel: a direct picture of how one state distributes later possibilities.
  2. Prefix-only peak Boundary changes, minimum-information-partition turnover, and effective-rank shifts selected one structural peak before outcomes opened.
  3. Prospective hit At those peaks, the later future map reorganized more than at neighboring checkpoints in 14 of 19 fresh families.
03 / discoveries

Four things survived every change of framing

These are now properties of the system worth studying in their own right, not consolation prizes for an earlier endpoint.

Controlled information mode

We can drive Φ-R up—and watch it relax

The orthogonal policy built a clear Φ+ versus Φ− separation during sixteen passages of active control. The effect vanished immediately after release. That gives us a response curve, a relaxation timescale, and a clean way to ask about dose and hysteresis.

Structural boundary

The rare canalizing cases lived where the decomposition broke form

Among 144 branching groups, all 119 fully estimable groups had negative canalization outcomes. The only two positive outcomes appeared among the 25 groups crossing an estimator boundary, both in family f19. That post-outcome anomaly gave us a precise structural place to look.

Anisotropic woundability

The system filters injury through state and direction

All 24 within-state direction pairs separated, but one global mass dose could not put every lesion on the same morphological shell. In f20, two nearly equal D4 wounds required 2.75% versus 7.25% matter removal. Damage is not a scalar input here; it is geometry.

05B / morphogenesis

Before the creature, the whole moved first

We returned to the most literal Levin-inspired question: can an integrated predictive configuration appear while a Flow Lenia seed is still becoming an organism? In 64 fresh source specimens, the frozen whole-over-parts signal rose before an independently certified sustained break from the artificial square.

+0.364fresh pre-event rise · 95% interval +0.231 to +0.502

The state was the square-root mass composition of four spatial quadrants. The primary score compared their joint next-step predictive information with the sum available after the best independent bipartition. Its event-near mean exceeded its own earlier pre-event mean in 39 of 54 specimens with complete 32-step windows.

The detector is conservative—it marks committed non-square organism formation, not the first faint internal texture. Ordinary whole-state predictability had no consistent direction, while the formal whole-over-parts-minus-TDMI contrast was positive on average but noisy.
06 / meaning

Organization may be an event, not an amount

The experiments now fit one sharper picture. A Flow Lenia system can become more predictable as a whole than as separable parts before committed morphology arrives; later, its information modes, effective parts, wounds, and possible futures expose further layers of organization. The interesting object is the transition architecture connecting those layers.

After release

A short memory survived the score.

Full Φ versus Φ− differed little in the scalar at passage 41 (+0.024) and not at passage 42 (−0.015). But the founder-composition path stayed longer under Φ+: +0.112 at passage 41, +0.093 at passage 42, and +0.037 at passage 45, before crossing near zero by passage 49.

09 / next

Now move the architecture

Prediction got us to the doorstep twice. The decisive experiments should now move the early whole-over-parts precursor, test whether organism birth moves with it, and ask whether the later structural event changes the future map.

01

Move the birth precursor

At matched early seed states, choose small perturbations that raise or lower whole-minus-parts while keeping mass, raw motion, and whole TDMI close. Test whether the certified morphology event advances or retreats.

02

Replicate beyond the fixed panel

Sample new families independently, use new future-seed sets, keep the selector frozen, and ask whether the structural-peak contrast survives across both families and future environments.

03

Induce and suppress the peak

Create matched interventions that promote or block the boundary/MIP/rank event while holding mass, founder composition, and ordinary predictability as close as possible. Then measure whether Q moves.

04

Ask whether futures contract

At peak and off-peak states, apply several state-aware, severity-matched wounds and launch fresh futures. The key outcome is whether injury fans lose their identity and converge toward one endpoint.

05

Separate controller from action sequence

Replay each closed-loop arm’s exact actions open-loop on matched states. If only feedback control preserves the information-bearing mode, the controller and ecology are making it together.

06

Target identity loss prospectively

On fresh families, freeze founder-loss time as the outcome and test whether susceptibility peaks forecast it beyond passage clock and ordinary composition. Replay the chosen actions open-loop to separate feedback control from the action sequence itself.

The observation we are now trying to earn An integrated predictive configuration helps create the organism; later structural transitions reorganize its counterfactual futures. Advancing or blocking those events moves the biology.

Preserved technical attempts and why they stopped
  • Recovery engineering v1: an occupied-support assertion fired before challenge exposure; no biological outcome opened.
  • Recovery engineering v2: a Float32 dose-accounting reduction disagreed after state installation; the state itself was intact.
  • Recovery engineering v3/v3a: deterministic mechanics completed, then the audit window was repaired prospectively without rerunning the biology.
  • Reservoir census v1: a grouped-mass reduction mismatch stopped the stage before outcomes; the corrected v2 replay closed 2,803 division events.
  • Steering smoke attempts 1–3: Float32 JSON encoding and a post-fork prefix digest were corrected before the final ten-run qualification.
  • Layout qualification v1: 2-channel, 10-kernel founders met a borrowed 3-channel, 20-kernel base; v2 switched to the founders’ native 2c10 base without resampling genotypes.
  • Compact evidence v1/v1b: two verifier assumptions—raw JSON key order and cross-accumulator mass equality—were replaced by canonical-record and native-accumulator checks.
  • Concurrency qualification v1: all 40 mechanical runs finished, but the diagnostic receipt could not certify the system-log slice; v2 used a hash-closed classifier and passed exact 8-way parity.
  • Connected-bite calibration: one preserved preparation abort preceded the successful 32-run calibration.
  • Matched-wound confirmation: preparation and execution aborts were preserved; the final 16 selected wounds completed and produced the directional map reported above.
External starting point

Pigozzi and Levin

The GARD causal-emergence paper supplied the core provocation: organization may become measurable before overt self-replication. Our estimator and Flow Lenia ontology are explicit adaptations, not a claim to reproduce their undisclosed implementation exactly.

Evidence boundary

Where we stand

We have prospective prediction of future-repertoire reorganization in a fixed panel. We have not yet shown that the structural peak causes that reorganization, that the changed map is contractive, or that an autonomous replicator results. Those are now concrete experiments rather than generic caveats.
