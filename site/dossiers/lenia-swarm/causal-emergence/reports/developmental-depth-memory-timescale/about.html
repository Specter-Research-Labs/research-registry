The Body's Response to an Earlier Intervention Reappears Later

The depth-specific order trace was absent at zero gap, formed after sixteen steps, changed layer and sign at later gaps, and returned strongly after 128 steps.

memory-timescale intervention2026-08-23

Question

Does the effect of the first intervention simply decay, or does the body pass through a sequence of different response states?

Result

The trace behaved as a delayed, recurrent process rather than a fading scar, with different gaps exposing inward and surface-biased responses.

Next question

Can continuous measurement locate the state transition that launches each later response without relying on a fixed elapsed time?
