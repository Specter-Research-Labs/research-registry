Intervention Order Outlasts the Final Pulse

Two four-pulse histories had the same zero net hidden-state dose but opposite order, and the early body followed the first pulse block rather than the last intervention.

zero-net causal-order intervention2026-08-23

Question

When total dose cancels exactly, does the order of hidden causes still direct development?

Result

It does; opposite orders produced different body futures, which rules out both simple dose integration and a last-pulse explanation for the observed direction.

Next question

Over which developmental ages does the body continue to understand this order as the same instruction?
