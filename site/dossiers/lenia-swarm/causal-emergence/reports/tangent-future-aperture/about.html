Information-Directed Pulses Favor a Dominant Body

In thirty-eight reused early states, one information-forward push was released into eight new futures and reduced disagreement in how much mass consolidated into the dominant connected component.

reused-cohort aperture exploration2026-08-25

Question

Can a brief information-directed intervention stabilize one feature of development without reducing the diversity of all later forms?

Result

The reused cohort showed narrower dominant-body allocation under the forward push, while other morphology remained diverse; this lead was kept separate from the later fresh fine-shape confirmation, which was null.

Next question

An independent cohort would need to use dominant-body allocation as its sole primary measurement before the futures are generated.
