02 / The information transition

This is not a scalar “more Φ” story.

Both readouts fall through the ignition window. But the causal arrow strengthens. The state is becoming less able to predict its immediate next state while becoming more asymmetrically able to steer its longer-term morphology.

Predictive information and causal leverage have separated. That separation may be the important event—not a peak in either score by itself.

04 / The emerging picture

Organism formation may be a transfer from prediction into directional control.

The initial state is highly predictable because it is still close to a constrained seed regime. As hidden composition differentiates and visible development accelerates, immediate predictability drops—but the system acquires an internally aligned direction with selective control over what body comes next.

A regime, not one magic variable

No single passage 12−passage 8 scalar strongly explains which organism gains the most causal strength; the largest rank association is only 0.185. The ignition is coordinated across several dimensions rather than reducible to one readout.

The Levin connection gets sharper

The important precursor may not be a rising information quantity. It may be a reorganization in how information relates to possible futures: less passive next-step prediction, more privileged long-horizon causal direction.

05 / The decisive intervention

Move a system across the ignition.

Observation has taken us as far as it should. The direct test is to transplant the passage 12 hidden organization into the same passage 8 visible body—and transplant passage 8 back into passage 12.

If passage 12-in-passage 8 advances the visible developmental turn and gives the passage 8 state passage 12-like causal leverage, while passage 8-in-passage 12 delays or weakens both, then we have located and manipulated a hidden pre-morphological state transition—not merely correlated with it.
