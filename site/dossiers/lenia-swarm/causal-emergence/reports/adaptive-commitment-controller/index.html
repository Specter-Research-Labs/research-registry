NEXT DIRECT SWING

Map the maintenance law.

Pulse frequency

Re-select every 2, 4, or 8 steps. Find the minimum feedback rate that holds the split, and whether the required rate changes near morphological transition.

Hold → release → rescue

Drive the state high, release it until the split decays, then resume adaptive control. Test hysteresis, recovery speed, and whether a formerly driven organism becomes easier to steer.

That would test how quickly the controller must update, how long its effects persist, and whether they can be reversed.
