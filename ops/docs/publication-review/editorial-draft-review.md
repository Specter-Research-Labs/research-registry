## Evidence and limits

The review cross-checked contradictions against the existing release ledger, the poly-morphogenesis implementation audit, the current controlled Wonton note, and the canonical Morphospace account. Lenia, Flow Lenia, MAP-Elites and isotropic-line primary-paper metadata/abstracts were checked directly at arXiv; the normalized line direction was also checked in `site/blog/lenia-explainer/lenia-explainer.js`. This was not a fresh replication of historical numerical cohorts. The tissue sweep tables deliberately remain historical and uncertified, rather than having their provenance erased.

No release status was promoted and no draft was deleted. The canonical routes now provide a reading order without combining incompatible cohorts. Historical plots can retain historical terminology only with the explanatory captions; newly published plots should use the corrected terminology directly. Automatic bibliography conversion of the existing overview/primer reading lists remains separate from this prose review; do not pretend unverified historical source descriptions have become verified citations merely by formatting them.
